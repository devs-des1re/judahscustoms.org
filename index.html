Judahs Customs

On the 13th of October 2026, the unthinkable happened: Judahs Customs is gone. Without warning, without any kind of notice to its staff or its members, the server was raided or deleted, and it now sits completely empty where a thriving community once stood. In the hours that followed, claims began circulating that Liquid may have taken the USD and group funds tied to the server before deactivating their account and disappearing entirely, leaving behind nothing but questions and a community that woke up to find everything they had built simply erased.

None of this has been confirmed. This is not an accusation, and no one is being called out or blamed here. These are simply claims that are currently being discussed and passed around within the community, based on what people have pieced together from the little information available. Until more concrete evidence surfaces, whether that comes from Discord itself, from former staff, or from anyone with real insight into what actually happened, everything being said right now should be treated as speculation rather than confirmed fact, and everyone is encouraged to keep that in mind before drawing their own conclusions.

What makes this so difficult to process is the sheer scale of it: over 3,700 members were reportedly gone in less than a minute. One of the biggest and most respected servers in the entire community simply ceased to exist in the time it takes to read this sentence. Years of conversations, memories, inside jokes, shared projects, and history were wiped out instantly, with no warning and no chance for anyone to save what mattered to them. For countless people, this server was never just a place to chat about customs and share work, it was where friendships were formed, where collaborations turned into real projects, and where a genuine, lasting community was built up from nothing over a very long period of time and effort.

Whether this turns out to have been an internal falling out between people who once trusted each other, a targeted raid from outside the server, a deliberate and calculated deletion, or something else entirely that hasn't even been considered yet, it is a loss that almost nobody in this community saw coming. Communities of this size, history, and standing do not simply disappear overnight without reason, and the suddenness of it all has left a great many people shocked, upset, and left searching for answers that may never fully come, no matter how much time passes or how many theories are discussed.

Judahs Customs was never just a server. It was a legacy. It represented years of consistent effort from its founders, its moderators, and every single member who ever showed up, contributed their time, shared their work, or simply stuck around to be part of something bigger than themselves. Its absence leaves a gap in this community that will not be easy to fill, and for many, no replacement server will ever quite capture what this one managed to build over the years it was active.

Right now, more than anything, we stand together in respect for everything Judahs Customs built and everything it stood for during its time. To everyone who was ever a part of it in any way, whether as a long-time member, a dedicated staff member, an active contributor, or simply someone who quietly watched it grow from the sidelines over the years, thank you for being part of its story and for helping make it what it was.

Rest in peace, Judahs Customs.
